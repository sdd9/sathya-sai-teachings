About the programme

What SSSE is

Sri Sathya Sai Spiritual Education is the name used outside India for Bal Vikas — “the blossoming of the child.” Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba began the programme so that children would not only become clever, but good: steady in love, useful to society, and true to their own conscience.

In 1995, at the Sixth World Conference of Sri Sathya Sai Organisations, the name Sai Spiritual Education (SSE) was adopted internationally to underline that the same human values belong to every culture. In India the original name Bal Vikas remains.

Ordinary education pours information in. Educare draws the values out. A guru’s work is to create the climate in which they can blossom: prayer, example, story, song, silence, and service.

The guru's mission

How to reveal the Sai in their hearts

“The children of the Sathya Sai Bala Vihars must know the Sathya Sai residing in their hearts. The teachers must also take it as the Puja of Sai Ram. How to reveal to these children the Sai, who is in their hearts—that is the problem you should set before yourselves. By leading the children towards Divinity, the teachers are serving their parents and society, for they will cleanse and brighten their homes and surroundings,” Baba said.
