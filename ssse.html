SRI SATHYA SAI SPIRITUAL EDUCATION

SSSE — Bal Vikas

Bal Vikas means the blossoming of the child. Outside India the same programme is called Sri Sathya Sai Spiritual Education (SSSE or SSE). It is a free weekly class in which children practise the five human values already present in them — Truth (Sathya), Right Conduct (Dharma), Peace (Shanti), Love (Prema), and Non-violence (Ahimsa).

Ordinary schooling pours facts in. Educare draws character out. A session weaves silent sitting, prayer, song, story, and a simple activity so that the head, heart, and hands move as one. Home remains the first classroom; the weekly hour only waters what parents tend every day.
